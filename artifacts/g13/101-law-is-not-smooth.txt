=== THE QUESTION THIS RUN WAS TAKEN TO ANSWER ===

The previous commit named two readings of note 60 being high in every fit, and
said five more repeats would discriminate:

  (a) one bad repeat -> the pooled row falls toward +2.82 and the fits improve
  (b) the law is not smooth in cv -> it holds near +2.91 and the next job is a
      cv sweep rather than a law

**IT IS (b).** Note 60 over nine repeats is +2.8837 +/- 0.0593, which moves the
row by 0.023 c, well inside its own error. **Every fit got WORSE, not better**,
because the error bar tightened while the residual did not move: note 60 is
+4.2 to +5.6 sd high depending on the law, and note 72 is 2.7 to 6.0 sd low.

  before (note 60 k=5):  proportional 6714/3   affine 52.6/2   quadratic 25.3/1
  after  (note 60 k=9):  proportional 6724/3   affine 71.5/2   quadratic 41.7/1

**n60-r2 is NOT the explanation.** It is 4.0 sd from its siblings, nothing
flags it, it was kept, and with nine repeats it is no longer able to carry the
residual on its own.

**No session-drift artefact either.** The two blocks were taken hours apart:
r1-r5 mean +2.9062 (sd 0.2054), r6-r10 mean +2.7791 (sd 0.2214). They differ by
0.127 c, inside their own spread.

=== WHAT THE NEXT MEASUREMENT IS ===

A cv SWEEP, not another law. If the shift is code-dependent rather than a
smooth function of cv, the object to measure is the per-code deviation, and a
6-bit DAC has only 64 codes. The four notes measured are cv 0, 24, 36 and 63.
Neighbouring codes are the discriminator: if cv 23 and cv 25 bracket cv 24's
high reading, the effect is local to the code and the answer is differential
nonlinearity. If they follow it, it is a smooth feature this sampling is too
coarse to see.

That is a G13 result rather than a defeat: "the rail shift read through this
DAC is not a smooth function of the code" is a statement a hardware model has
to reproduce, and it is stronger than a fitted slope.

=== A DEFECT IN MY OWN POOL, FOUND BY THIS RUN ===

`screen` sets `flagged` on a run that loses more than --max-drop-frac of its
captures and prints "a screen doing that much work is not cleaning a run, it is
telling you the run is broken". **`pool` was ignoring the field**, which made
that sentence decorative.

the published row by 0.041 c, comparable to its own error bar, on the authority
