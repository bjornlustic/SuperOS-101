=== THE TEST ===

The claim's surviving shape is a shift monotone in cv over widely spaced rows.
That predicts EVERY adjacent-note boundary steps the same small way, +0.05 to
+0.09 c in the positive 63-cv direction. A multi-bit carry in an R-2R ladder is
where DNL lives, so if the anomaly at 63-cv 39/40 (-0.79 c, published) is DNL
and not noise, the OTHER carries should misbehave and the LSB-only boundaries
should not.

  63-cv              bits    flip   kind    step c     +/-   sigma vs +0.07
  31-> 32  011111->100000       6  MAJOR   -0.4270  0.1682            -3.0
  39-> 40  100111->101000       4  MAJOR   -0.7915  0.1619            -5.3
  47-> 48  101111->110000       5  MAJOR   -0.7065  0.8098            -1.0
  38-> 39  100110->100111       1    LSB   +0.1462  0.2314            +0.3
  42-> 43  101010->101011       1    LSB   +0.5305  0.8442            +0.5

Errors are stat + harmonic + window systematics in quadrature, the FULL
early-late spread (the conservative convention; halving it, which is the more
standard choice, strengthens every line). 39/40 and 38/39 are the previously
published rows; 31/32, 47/48 and 42/43 are new here.

ALL THREE multi-bit carries are negative where the trend demands positive.
BOTH LSB-only boundaries are positive and consistent with the trend.

=== THE SIGN SURVIVES EVERY ESTIMATOR SETTING, WHICH IS THE STRONGER TEST ===

The pooled errors above are dominated by note 51 and note 57, and understate
how stable the SIGN is. Same captures, six estimator settings:

  MAJOR 31->32    ref -0.427  h8 -0.497  h32 -0.387  early -0.408  late -0.395  vlate -0.419
  MAJOR 47->48    ref -0.707  h8 -0.523  h32 -0.412  early -0.384  late -0.559  vlate -1.034
  ordinary 42->43 ref +0.530              early +0.522  late +1.110  vlate +1.203

Six of six negative at 31/32, six of six negative at 47/48, four of four
positive at 42/43. (h8 and h32 do not resolve note 57, so 42/43 has four.)

=== WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY ===

DOES: the 63-cv 39/40 anomaly is NOT a one-off. The boundaries that misbehave
are exactly the multi-bit carries and the boundaries that behave are exactly
the LSB-only ones, across five boundaries and six estimator settings. That is
differential nonlinearity at the ladder's carries, and it is why no low-order
law in (63-cv) fits the rows: the curve has steps in it at the carries.

DOES NOT: give a magnitude law. The three anomalies are -0.43, -0.71, -0.79 c
and their errors DO NOT SEPARATE THEM. In particular the MSB carry (six bits
changing, 63-cv 31->32) has the SMALLEST anomaly and the four-bit carry the
largest, which is inverted from the naive R-2R expectation that the MSB is
worst; but 31/32 and 39/40 differ by 0.36 +/- 0.23, which is 1.6 sigma and
establishes nothing. NO ORDERING BY BIT POSITION IS CLAIMED.

THE WEAK LEG, stated plainly: 47/48 is -1.0 sigma on its own. Note 51 is the
unstable cell in this whole set (window systematic 0.72 c, between-repeat sd
0.37 c) and it carries that boundary. The sign is six-for-six across variants,
but the magnitude there is not measured. A fourth repeat (mc-r7) is running to
tighten note 51 and this file will be amended with it.

THE SECOND CONTROL IS THE PRECISE ONE. 42/43 is +0.53 +/- 0.84, which is
consistent with the trend and also consistent with almost anything. The
LSB-only control that actually has resolution is 38/39 at +0.146 +/- 0.231,
correct sign, consistent with +0.07. Do not quote 42/43 as if it were tight.

=== MACHINE STATE, BRACKETED (L136) ===

Every run in this pool was bracketed by a boot-counter probe read:

  before mc-r5  MCUSR 0b01000  boots 17  vcc 4984 mV
  before mc-r6  MCUSR 0b01000  boots 17  vcc 4984 mV     (= after mc-r5)
  after  mc-r6  MCUSR 0b01000  boots 17  vcc 4984 mV

boots did not move across ~25 minutes of capture load, and MCUSR still reports
the SAME old WDRF that was already there. mc-r5 is attributable: no reset
during it. This is what the bracket was added for and it worked.

TWO OPERATIONAL FACTS FOUND THE HARD WAY, recorded so they are not rediscovered:

1. mc-r6 DIED OF A HOST AUDIO WEDGE, NOT THE MACHINE. ffmpeg hung on a single
   `-t 3.0` avfoundation capture, leaving a ZERO-BYTE wav, and stayed hung for
   4+ minutes ignoring SIGTERM; only SIGKILL cleared it. The SSL device stayed
   enumerated throughout. The bracket read boots 17 across it, so THE CAPTURE
   PATH CAN FAIL WITH THE MACHINE NOT RESETTING. That is a real discriminator
   and it is the competing explanation for mc-r3 that I previously could not
   separate. It does NOT show r3 was host-side: r3's signature was a level and
   timbre collapse, not a zero-byte file. It shows the failure mode exists.

2. THE `after` PROBE OF mc-r5 PRINTED NOTHING AND THAT LOOKS EXACTLY LIKE A
   CLEAN READ. bracket.sh pipes the probe through `grep -E "raw\[  0\]|..."`
   with 2>&1, so a failed probe has its error swallowed and leaves an EMPTY
   section under the heading. An empty bracket reads as no news when it is
   actually no measurement. The r5 bracket survives only because the
   `before mc-r6` read is taken after r5 finished and serves the same purpose.
   Fix before the next bracketed run: check the probe's exit status, and print
   FAILED rather than nothing.
