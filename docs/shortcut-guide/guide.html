Blue dashed box: any key in that group.
LEDslit  blinking  off
On the photo a lit LED glows red; a blinking one is half lit inside a dashed red ring. The box beside each drawing repeats the seven LEDs large. Keys are numbered 1 to 32 from the lowest F; key 8 is the lowest C.

Playing
1

Keyboard

KeyboardNothing lit

No LED: plain keyboard mode. Keys 8 and 15 held, key 8 (the lower) sounds.

  1. With no function LED lit, the keyboard plays the voice directly.
  2. Hold two keys: the lower one sounds. Let it go and the other one takes over.
  3. Key 1 is the lowest F, key 8 the lowest C, key 32 the top C.
NoteWith the ENV TRIG switch on GATE+TRIG the priority is the last key pressed instead. MIDI notes 53 to 84 act as keys 1 to 32.
2

Hold

Keyboard or arpeggioAny time

HOLD lit while the button (or the pedal) is down: the keys played keep sounding.

  1. Keep HOLD pressed, or press the HOLD pedal.
  2. Play keys: they keep sounding after you let go. A new chord replaces the latched one.
  3. Release HOLD (or the pedal): the latched keys are released and the LED goes out.
NoteThe HOLD LED follows the button and the pedal: it is lit only while one of them is down. A pedal on the HOLD jack is the hands-free way to latch.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide3
3

Transpose Switch

KeyboardAny time

No LED. The switch is read with every key.

  1. Set TRANSPOSE to L, M or H: the keyboard plays an octave down, as marked, or an octave up.
  2. Keys you record take the octave the switch is at; a stored pattern keeps it.
NoteThe TRANSPOSE switch works on keys, the arpeggio and recording, as on the stock SH-101. While a sequence plays it picks the performance row instead (Performance Modes). Tune (below) assumes M.
4

Portamento Auto

Keyboard, arpeggio, sequencerSwitch on AUTO

No LED. Key 12 pressed while key 8 is held: legato, so it glides.

  1. Set the PORTAMENTO switch to AUTO and the PORTAMENTO knob to the glide time.
  2. Play legato (the next key before you let go of the last): the pitch glides.
  3. Play detached: each note starts on its own pitch.
NoteThe firmware pulses PORTAMENTO OFF on every fresh note, so on AUTO only legato keys and legato (tied) sequencer steps glide. ON and OFF ignore it, as stock.

Arpeggio
5

Arpeggio

ArpeggioFrom keyboard mode

UP lit: arpeggio up over keys 8, 12, 15.

  1. Press UP, DOWN or U&D: its LED lights.
  2. Hold a chord: the notes play one after another at the LFO / CLK RATE (or the MIDI clock with sync on).
  3. Press another direction to change it; press the lit button again to go back to the keyboard.
NoteEach arpeggio note retriggers the envelope; CV / GATE OUT and MIDI OUT follow it.
6

Random Arpeggio

ArpeggioFrom keyboard or arpeggio

U&D blinking: random arpeggio.

  1. Press DOWN and U&D together: the U&D LED blinks.
  2. Held keys play in random order, never the same note twice in a row when there is a choice.
  3. DOWN + U&D together again: back to the keyboard.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide5
7

Arpeggio Octave Range

ArpeggioArpeggio on

UP still lit: the range changed, the mode did not. No LED shows the range.

  1. With an arpeggio on, hold KEY TRANSPOSE.
  2. UP adds an octave, DOWN takes one away: 1 to 3 octaves.
  3. UP climbs octave by octave, DOWN starts at the top, U&D bounces across the whole span, random picks an octave too.
NoteThe range is kept across power-off. It needs an arpeggio running: in keyboard mode KEY TRANSPOSE + UP does nothing to the range.
8

Arpeggio and Hold

ArpeggioArpeggio on

U&D and HOLD lit: the chord keeps arpeggiating after the keys are released.

  1. Pick an arpeggio direction.
  2. Keep HOLD down, or press the pedal, and play the chord: it keeps running with the keys released.
  3. A new chord replaces it. In this state transpose mode (Transpose chapter) moves it with single keys.

Sequencer
9

Record a Pattern

LOADFrom stop

LOAD lit: recording. Two steps entered.

  1. Stopped, press LOAD: the LED lights and the current pattern is cleared.
  2. Play the notes, one key per step. Timing does not matter: every key is one step.
  3. Press LOAD again: the pattern is stored and the LED goes out.
NoteUp to 128 steps, 16 patterns, kept across power-off. PLAY + key 1-16 picks the pattern to record into (Pattern Select).
10

Ties, Rests, Ratchets, Undo

LOADRecording

LOAD lit. Recorded: key 8, key 10 tied, a rest, key 12 (then ratcheted and taken back).

  1. Hold HOLD while pressing a key: that step is legato (no retrigger, glides on AUTO).
  2. KEY TRANSPOSE enters a rest.
  3. UP cycles the last step's ratchet: 1x, 2x, 3x hits inside the step.
  4. DOWN takes the last step back; press it again to go further back.
  5. Hold U&D while pressing a key: an accented step (needs the board's filter and accent wires).
NoteThe stock panel prints LEGATO under HOLD and REST under KEY TRANSPOSE for this.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide7
11

Play and Stop

Sequencer playKeyboard mode

PLAY lit: the pattern runs at the LFO / CLK RATE.

  1. Press PLAY: the LED lights and step 1 sounds at once, the next steps at the LFO / CLK RATE (or the MIDI clock with sync on).
  2. Press PLAY again to stop.
NoteWith MIDI sync on, PLAY only arms: the first step waits for the clock (MIDI Sync).
12

Correct and Add

LOADPlaying

LOAD lit: recording again from the step after the one that was sounding.

  1. While the pattern plays, press LOAD. Playback stops and the LOAD LED lights.
  2. The keys overwrite from the step after the one that was sounding. Steps after the ones you correct stay as they were. If the last step was sounding, the new steps go after it.
  3. LOAD again stores.
NoteThe stock SH-101's Correcting and Adding. LOAD from stop starts a new pattern instead.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide8
13

Real-Time Record

Sequencer playPlaying

PLAY and LOAD alternating: real-time record.

  1. While playing, hold PLAY and press LOAD: the PLAY and LOAD LEDs alternate.
  2. Keys overwrite the step they land on (the next step once the step is half over). A key held past the half-step ties into the next step. KEY TRANSPOSE writes a rest.
  3. Hold PLAY and press LOAD again to end. The edits are stored when playback stops.
NoteAn empty pattern gets 16 rest steps to record into.
14

Pattern Select

SequencerStopped or playing

Pattern 2 picked from stop: it plays when PLAY is released.

  1. Hold PLAY and press one of the 16 lowest keys (key 1 = pattern 1).
  2. From stop, the pattern starts when you release PLAY.
  3. While playing, it follows at the end of the current pass.
NoteMIDI program change 0 to 15 does the same. Hold PLAY and press nothing to toggle play as usual. With PLAY held, UP / DOWN / U&D randomize and KEY TRANSPOSE sets the scale (Randomize and Scale).
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide9
15

Song

SequencerStopped or playing

Song 1, 2, 1, 3 started from stop.

  1. Hold PLAY and press several of keys 1-16 in the order to play them.
  2. Release PLAY: from stop the song starts; while playing it starts at the end of the pass.
  3. Each pattern plays once, then the next; the song loops.
NotePicking a single pattern, or an instant switch, ends the song.
16

Instant Switch

Sequencer play, SuperOS modePlaying, HOLD LED off

PLAY lit, HOLD off (SuperOS mode). Pattern 2 took over at the same position.

  1. In SuperOS mode (HOLD + LOAD, HOLD LED off), press one of keys 1-16 while playing.
  2. That pattern takes over at the next step, at the same position (modulo its length).
NotePatterns whose lengths are multiples of each other stay in step. In performance mode row 1 keys 25-31 switch patterns 1-7 the same way.

17

Play Direction

Sequencer playPlaying

PLAY stays lit: the arpeggio LEDs do not show the direction.

  1. While playing, with PLAY not held, press UP (forward), DOWN (backward), U&D (ping-pong), or DOWN and U&D together (random).
  2. Ping-pong repeats its end steps (1 to n, then n to 1), so a round trip is 2n steps and stays on the bar.
  3. The direction is kept across power-off.
NoteConfig menu keys 15-18 set it too. With PLAY held these buttons randomize the pattern instead (Randomize and Scale).
18

Shift

Sequencer playPlaying

PLAY lit; the pattern moved one step ahead of the clock.

  1. While playing, hold KEY TRANSPOSE.
  2. UP plays the next step at once, DOWN the previous one: the pattern moves against the clock.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide11
19

Nudge

Sequencer playPlaying

PLAY lit (HOLD lights only while held). The notes moved, the timing did not.

  1. While playing, hold the HOLD button.
  2. UP moves every note one step right (later), DOWN one step left (earlier). Ratchets and accents move with their steps; the pattern wraps around.
  3. The move lands at the next half-step: the sounding note has already started and the next one is not chosen yet, so nothing is cut or doubled and the beat never moves.
NoteUnlike Shift, which moves the playhead against the clock, Nudge moves the notes inside the pattern. The web editor has the same as ◀ Nudge / Nudge ▶ (Shift + arrow keys), with Undo.
20

Loop Length, Live

Sequencer playPlaying

Key 2 with HOLD held: only the first 2 steps loop.

  1. While playing, hold the HOLD button and press key n: only the first n steps loop (key 1 = 1 step).
  2. The same key again: the full pattern.
NoteNothing is erased. The HOLD pedal does not do this; it keeps its hold (or transpose) job.

Randomize and Scale
21

Randomize

SequencerStopped or playing

PLAY stays lit: a new pattern while playing. No LED shows a randomize.

  1. Hold PLAY and press U&D: a new pattern, rhythm and pitch.
  2. PLAY held + UP: new pitches, the rhythm stays. PLAY held + DOWN: mutate, 2 to 4 small edits.
  3. It works stopped or playing and never starts play. Written notes land in the pattern's scale when one is on. UP and DOWN need a pattern with steps.
NoteUnder PLAY held these buttons no longer switch the arpeggio or the direction. The current pattern is changed in place and stored like any edit.
22

Scale

SequencerStopped or playing

PLAY stays lit: C root, second scale. No LED shows the scale.

  1. Hold PLAY and KEY TRANSPOSE and press a key: that note is the root, with Major. Key 8 = C.
  2. Still holding KEY TRANSPOSE, press the same key again to step through the 35 scales, Major first.
  3. Hold PLAY and tap KEY TRANSPOSE alone: scale on / off.
NoteThe scale belongs to the pattern and is stored with it. It is non-destructive: the output snaps to the nearest scale note (the lower one on a tie) before any transpose, and the steps themselves are unchanged. Under PLAY held, KEY TRANSPOSE neither transposes nor counts toward the transpose-mode double tap.

Performance Modes
23

Performance / SuperOS Mode

Any mode but LOADAny time

PLAY lit, HOLD lit: performance mode, the keys are the overlay.

  1. Hold HOLD and press LOAD: performance mode on or off. The choice is saved.
  2. Performance mode: while a sequence plays the HOLD LED stays lit and the keys are the performance rows. The HOLD LED flashes three times when you switch it on.
  3. SuperOS mode: HOLD LED off (one flash when you switch), the keys switch patterns 1-16 while playing.
NoteRatchet recording, randomize and mutate (PLAY + UP / DOWN / U&D), nudge, shift, loop length and the scale gestures work in both modes: they use a held function button. Performance choices keep playing in SuperOS mode until reset.
24

Performance Rows

Sequencer playPlaying

PLAY lit. TRANSPOSE on M: the keys are row 2, Pitch.

  1. While a sequence plays, the keys are the overlay strip above them.
  2. The TRANSPOSE switch picks the row: L = 1 Global, M = 2 Pitch, H = 3 Time.
  3. A choice stays on until you press the same key again or reset it. Nothing is written into the pattern.
NoteOnly while playing with no function button held: HOLD + key, PLAY + key and KEY TRANSPOSE + key keep their jobs. Stopped, the keys play notes and the switch sets the octave. The web editor can turn the overlay off.

25

Row 1: Global

Sequencer playPlaying, TRANSPOSE on L

KEY TRANSPOSE lit: key 10 transposed the pattern up 2, from the next step.

  1. Keys 1-12 transpose from the lowest C (key 8 = none), from the next step on.
  2. Keys 13-19: octave variations 1-7 (7 = a random octave each step).
  3. Keys 20-24: the LFO (and its random S&H) restarts on a 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 or 1/1 grid. The same key again stops the restart.
  4. Keys 25-31: patterns 1-7, switched at the next step, at the same position.
NoteKey 32 alone clears the row (transpose, octaves, LFO sync); held + a key turns that function off. Every choice lands on the next step, never on the sounding note.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide15
26

Row 2: Pitch

Sequencer playPlaying, TRANSPOSE on M

PLAY lit. Key 2: the notes play in reverse order, the rhythm is kept.

  1. Keys 1-7 reorder the notes (rests stay): as written, reverse, 1st 3rd 5th first, 2nd 4th 6th first, a prime stride, and one forward / one back, which move one note further every loop pass.
  2. Keys 8-19 set the quantizer root, keys 20-26 the type: major, minor, dorian, harmonic minor, lydian, phrygian, locrian. The quantizer acts after reorder and combine, so it scales the combined notes too. Its key again turns the scale off, and on again.
  3. Key 27 cycles the partner pattern through the saved ones; keys 28-31 combine the pitches with it: add, subtract, divide, difference.
NoteA root alone uses major, a type alone uses C. With no partner picked, it is the next pattern. Key 32 alone clears the row; key 32 held + a key turns that function off.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide16
27

Row 3: Time

Sequencer playPlaying, TRANSPOSE on H

PLAY lit. Key 5: the pattern plays a sixteenth later.

  1. Keys 1-7 shift time: 1/4 right, left; 1/8 right, left; 1/16 right, left; 3/16 right.
  2. Keys 8-12 skip every 4th, every 8th, even, odd or every 5th clock: silent, the pattern waits.
  3. Keys 13-17 pick a grid (1/4 1/8 1/16 1/4T 1/16T), keys 18-21 how it meets the step clock (NOR OR AND XOR): OR adds steps, AND deletes them.
  4. Key 27 cycles the rhythm partner through the saved patterns; keys 28-31 combine the rhythm with it: AND OR NOR XOR.
NoteThe grid follows the MIDI clock when it drives the SH-101. Ratchet (keys 22-26) is on the next page. Key 32 alone clears the row.

28

Ratchet

Sequencer playPlaying, TRANSPOSE on H

PLAY lit. Key 23 held: from the next step its note repeats every sixteenth.

  1. Hold one of keys 22-26: from the next step its note repeats at 1/32, 1/16, 1/16 triplets, 3 or 7 times a step.
  2. The pattern runs on underneath: let go and it carries on where it would be.
NoteRatchet is the one function that does not latch.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide18
29

Write and Reset

Sequencer playPlaying

PLAY lit: still playing, now from the written pattern.

  1. KEY TRANSPOSE + LOAD while playing writes one pass of what plays into the pattern: reorder, quantizer, combine, octave variation, shift and transpose become its notes. An active scale stays on as the pattern's own.
  2. Those settings are then cleared, so the new pattern is the baseline and sounds the same. The LFO/S&H sync and the timing ones (skip, add/delete, ratchet) stay on.
  3. Key 32 tapped clears its row; held for 1 second it clears all three rows.
NoteWriting changes the stored pattern; everything else in the overlay never does.

Transpose and Tune
30

Key Transpose

Any mode but LOADAny time

KEY TRANSPOSE lit: transposed up 4 semitones (key 12 = E).

  1. Hold KEY TRANSPOSE and press a key: everything that plays moves by its distance from the lowest C (key 8). The LED stays lit.
  2. It stays after you let go: keyboard, arpeggio and sequencer all follow.
  3. KEY TRANSPOSE + key 8 returns to normal and the LED goes out.
NoteUnlike the stock SH-101 the transpose also reaches CV OUT and MIDI OUT.
31

Transpose Mode

Sequencer play or held arpeggioAny time

KEY TRANSPOSE blinking: transpose mode. Key 10 moved the pattern up 2.

  1. Tap KEY TRANSPOSE twice quickly: its LED blinks.
  2. While the sequencer plays, or an arpeggio runs latched by HOLD, plain keys transpose: no button to hold.
  3. Double-tap again to leave; the last transpose stays.

32

Pedal as Key Transpose

Config menu, then any modeSet once

Pedal down + key 12 after key 19: KEY TRANSPOSE lit, transposed.

  1. In the config menu press key 19: the HOLD pedal jack now acts as the KEY TRANSPOSE button.
  2. Hold the pedal and press a key to transpose; double-tap it for transpose mode; in LOAD it enters a rest.
  3. Key 19 again in the menu: back to HOLD.
NoteKept across power-off. The HOLD button keeps working as HOLD.
33

Tune

KeyboardKeyboard mode

LOAD blinking: the tune note is sounding.

  1. From keyboard mode, hold KEY TRANSPOSE and press LOAD: the LOAD LED blinks and an A sounds with the gate open.
  2. At 8' with TRANSPOSE on M it is 440 Hz; MIDI OUT sends note 69 for a tuner.
  3. Set TUNE, then press any function button to leave.

Config Menu
34

Enter and Leave

Config menuAny time

Menu: HOLD lit; PLAY lit = SuperOS is running.

  1. Hold HOLD and KEY TRANSPOSE together for 2 seconds (or while switching on).
  2. The HOLD LED lights, the gate closes, and LOAD (original firmware) or PLAY (SuperOS) shows the firmware that is running. KEY TRANSPOSE lit = MIDI clock sync on.
  3. Press KEY TRANSPOSE to leave. Every setting is kept across power-off.
35

Firmware: SuperOS or Original

Config menuIn the menu

PLAY lit: SuperOS is running.

  1. In the menu, LOAD switches to the original firmware: the instrument's original program.
  2. PLAY switches to SuperOS. The switch is immediate.
  3. The choice is kept; the menu works the same way in both.
NoteWith the original firmware the SH-101 behaves as from the factory (100 steps, stock arpeggio), with MIDI and clock sync added. Its power-on test mode (LOAD + KEY TRANSPOSE) works there.
36

MIDI Clock Sync

Config menuIn the menu

KEY TRANSPOSE lit: MIDI clock sync is on.

  1. In the menu, press U&D to switch MIDI clock sync on or off.
  2. KEY TRANSPOSE lit = on. The arpeggio and sequencer then follow MIDI clock (MIDI Sync).

Clock Division

Config menuIn the menu

Key 7: one step per 1/16 (the default).

  1. In the menu, press one of keys 1-10 for the step length: 1/4, 1/4 dotted, 1/4 triplet, 1/8, 1/8 dotted, 1/8 triplet, 1/16, 1/16 dotted, 1/16 triplet, 1/32.
  2. Or step through them with UP and DOWN.
NoteNo LED shows the division. It applies with MIDI sync on.

38

Accent, Cutoff, LFO Sync

Config menuIn the menu

Key 12: resonant accent.

  1. Key 11 / 12: accent model for notes at MIDI velocity 100 or more and accented steps: envelope (follows the synth's envelope) or resonant (a short sweep that builds up over accents).
  2. Key 13: MIDI filter cutoff (CC 74) on / off.
  3. Key 14: LFO hard-sync on / off. On: with MIDI sync the LFO is reset to the clock. Off: the LFO runs free for modulation while the steps still follow the clock.
NoteAccent and cutoff need the board's filter and accent wires. No LED shows these settings.
39

Direction and Pedal

Config menuIn the menu

Key 17: ping-pong.

  1. Keys 15-18 set the sequencer direction: forward, backward, ping-pong (the end steps repeat), random (the same setting as the buttons while playing).
  2. Key 19 switches the pedal jack between HOLD and KEY TRANSPOSE.

40

Sync Delay

Config menuIn the menu

Key 24: 8 ms later than the clock.

  1. Keys 20-31 set how much later than the incoming MIDI clock the SH-101 plays: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18, 21, 25 or 30 ms.
  2. Use it to line the SH-101 up with slower gear or a delayed listening path.
NoteOnly with MIDI sync on. The editor can set 0 to 60 ms.
41

Start Mode and MIDI Thru

Config menuIn the menu

Menu LEDs unchanged: no LED shows these settings.

  1. HOLD switches the start mode. Host (the default): the host's MIDI Start plays the SH-101. Armed: Start plays only a pattern you queued with PLAY; Stop sends it back to step 1, still queued.
  2. Key 32 switches the MIDI OUT jack: OUT sends the notes the SH-101 plays, THRU passes on what arrives at MIDI IN and sends none of its own notes.
NoteBoth are kept across power-off. Start mode acts only with MIDI sync on. The web editor shows and sets both.

MIDI Sync
42

Host Transport

Sequencer, sync onMIDI clock sync on

PLAY lit: the host's Start started the sequencer.

  1. With sync on, MIDI Start starts the sequencer from the first clock, MIDI Stop stops it.
  2. Pressing PLAY yourself only arms it: the first step waits for the host's clock.
  3. A synced SH-101 never runs on its own: no clock, no steps. The arpeggio follows the clock too.
NoteStart does not interrupt a running arpeggio or LOAD. With sync off, Start only rewinds a pattern that is already playing. While the clock drives the SH-101, the KEY TRANSPOSE LED blinks on the beat. Start mode Armed (Config Menu HOLD) changes step 1: see Start Mode and MIDI Thru.

MIDI

Notes 53 to 84 on the receive channel act as keys 1 to 32 (they arpeggiate and record); other notes 36 to 99 play the voice. Program change 0 to 15 selects pattern 1 to 16. CC 74 moves the filter cutoff and velocity 100 or more accents a note (with the board's filter and accent wires). Everything played goes out on MIDI OUT at velocity 100, accented steps at 127. The web editor edits patterns and settings over USB-C or DIN.

Resync

The editor's Resync button puts the sequence back on the one. It has no panel gesture. Direction, loop length, pattern switches and clock-division changes never move the sequence off the grid.
