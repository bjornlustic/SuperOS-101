Every SuperOS function on one drawing each: the panel, the controls involved, the order to press them, and the LEDs the SH-101 shows while you do it. The LED states are real: the firmware ran each sequence in the host simulator. Find the function, look at the picture, follow the numbered steps.

Reading the drawingsBlack ring: press this.
Red ring and red label: hold it while you do the rest.
Blue dashed box: any key in that group.
LEDslit  blinking  off
On the photo a lit LED glows red; a blinking one is half lit inside a dashed red ring. The box beside each drawing repeats the seven LEDs large. Keys are numbered 1 to 32 from the lowest F; key 8 is the lowest C.
WordsA + B: hold A, then press B. Tap: press and release. Playing: the sequencer runs. Clock: the LFO / CLK RATE, or MIDI clock with sync on. The header gives the mode and when it works.

Playing
1

Keyboard

KeyboardNothing lit

No LED: plain keyboard mode. Keys 8 and 15 held, key 8 (the lower) sounds.

  1. With no function LED lit, the keyboard plays the voice directly.
  2. Hold two keys: the lower one sounds. Let it go and the other one takes over.
  3. Key 1 is the lowest F, key 8 the lowest C, key 32 the top C.
NoteWith the ENV TRIG switch on GATE+TRIG the priority is the last key pressed instead. MIDI notes 53 to 84 act as keys 1 to 32.
2

Hold

Keyboard or arpeggioAny time

HOLD lit while the button (or the pedal) is down: the keys played keep sounding.

  1. Keep HOLD pressed, or press the HOLD pedal.
  2. Play keys: they keep sounding after you let go. A new chord replaces the latched one.
  3. Release HOLD (or the pedal): the latched keys are released and the LED goes out.
NoteThe HOLD LED follows the button and the pedal: it is lit only while one of them is down. A pedal on the HOLD jack is the hands-free way to latch.

3

Transpose Switch

KeyboardAny time

No LED. The switch is read with every key.

  1. Set TRANSPOSE to L, M or H: the keyboard plays an octave down, as marked, or an octave up.
  2. Keys you record take the octave the switch is at; a stored pattern keeps it.
NoteThe TRANSPOSE switch works on keys, the arpeggio and recording, as on the stock SH-101. Tune (below) assumes M.
4

Portamento Auto

Keyboard, arpeggio, sequencerSwitch on AUTO

No LED. Key 12 pressed while key 8 is held: legato, so it glides.

  1. Set the PORTAMENTO switch to AUTO and the PORTAMENTO knob to the glide time.
  2. Play legato (the next key before you let go of the last): the pitch glides.
  3. Play detached: each note starts on its own pitch.
NoteThe firmware pulses PORTAMENTO OFF on every fresh note, so on AUTO only legato keys and legato (tied) sequencer steps glide. ON and OFF ignore it, as stock.

Arpeggio
5

Arpeggio

ArpeggioFrom keyboard mode

UP lit: arpeggio up over keys 8, 12, 15.

  1. Press UP, DOWN or U&D: its LED lights.
  2. Hold a chord: the notes play one after another at the LFO / CLK RATE (or the MIDI clock with sync on).
  3. Press another direction to change it; press the lit button again to go back to the keyboard.
NoteEach arpeggio note retriggers the envelope; CV / GATE OUT and MIDI OUT follow it.
6

Random Arpeggio

ArpeggioFrom keyboard or arpeggio

U&D blinking: random arpeggio.

  1. Press DOWN and U&D together: the U&D LED blinks.
  2. Held keys play in random order, never the same note twice in a row when there is a choice.
  3. DOWN + U&D together again: back to the keyboard.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide5
7

Arpeggio Octave Range

ArpeggioArpeggio on

UP still lit: the range changed, the mode did not. No LED shows the range.

  1. With an arpeggio on, hold KEY TRANSPOSE.
  2. UP adds an octave, DOWN takes one away: 1 to 3 octaves.
  3. UP climbs octave by octave, DOWN starts at the top, U&D bounces across the whole span, random picks an octave too.
NoteThe range is kept across power-off. It needs an arpeggio running: in keyboard mode KEY TRANSPOSE + UP does nothing to the range.
8

Arpeggio and Hold

ArpeggioArpeggio on

U&D and HOLD lit: the chord keeps arpeggiating after the keys are released.

  1. Pick an arpeggio direction.
  2. Keep HOLD down, or press the pedal, and play the chord: it keeps running with the keys released.
  3. A new chord replaces it. In this state transpose mode (Transpose chapter) moves it with single keys.

Sequencer
9

Record a Pattern

LOADFrom stop

LOAD lit: recording. Two steps entered.

  1. Stopped, press LOAD: the LED lights and the current pattern is cleared.
  2. Play the notes, one key per step. Timing does not matter: every key is one step.
  3. Press LOAD again: the pattern is stored and the LED goes out.
NoteUp to 128 steps, 8 patterns, kept in flash across power-off. PLAY + key 1-8 picks the pattern to record into (Pattern Select).
10

Ties, Rests, Ratchets, Undo

LOADRecording

LOAD lit. Recorded: key 8, key 10 tied, a rest, key 12 (then ratcheted and taken back).

  1. Hold HOLD while pressing a key: that step is legato (no retrigger, glides on AUTO).
  2. KEY TRANSPOSE enters a rest.
  3. UP cycles the last step's ratchet: 1x, 2x, 3x hits inside the step.
  4. DOWN takes the last step back; press it again to go further back.
NoteThe stock panel prints LEGATO under HOLD and REST under KEY TRANSPOSE for this.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide7
11

Play and Stop

Sequencer playKeyboard mode

PLAY lit: the pattern runs at the LFO / CLK RATE.

  1. Press PLAY: the LED lights and step 1 sounds at once, the next steps at the LFO / CLK RATE (or the MIDI clock with sync on).
  2. Press PLAY again to stop.
NoteWith MIDI sync on, PLAY only arms: the first step waits for the clock (MIDI Sync).
12

Correct and Add

LOADPlaying

LOAD lit: recording again from the step after the one that was sounding.

  1. While the pattern plays, press LOAD. Playback stops and the LOAD LED lights.
  2. The keys overwrite from the step after the one that was sounding. Steps after the ones you correct stay as they were.
  3. LOAD again stores.
NoteThe stock SH-101's Correcting and Adding. LOAD from stop starts a new pattern instead.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide8
13

Real-Time Record

Sequencer playPlaying

PLAY and LOAD alternating: real-time record.

  1. While playing, hold PLAY and press LOAD: the PLAY and LOAD LEDs alternate.
  2. Keys overwrite the step they land on (the next step once the step is half over). A key held past the half-step ties into the next step. KEY TRANSPOSE writes a rest.
  3. Hold PLAY and press LOAD again to end. The edits are stored when playback stops.
NoteAn empty pattern gets 16 rest steps to record into.
14

Pattern Select

SequencerStopped or playing

Pattern 2 picked from stop: it plays when PLAY is released.

  1. Hold PLAY and press one of the 8 lowest keys (key 1 = pattern 1).
  2. From stop, the pattern starts when you release PLAY.
  3. While playing, it follows at the end of the current pass.
NoteMIDI program change 0 to 7 does the same. Hold PLAY and press nothing to toggle play as usual.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide9
15

Song

SequencerStopped or playing

Song 1, 2, 1, 3 started from stop.

  1. Hold PLAY and press several of keys 1-8 in the order to play them.
  2. Release PLAY: from stop the song starts; while playing it starts at the end of the pass.
  3. Each pattern plays once, then the next; the song loops.
NotePicking a single pattern, or an instant switch, ends the song.
16

Instant Switch

Sequencer playPlaying

Pattern 2 took over at the same position.

  1. While playing, press one of keys 1-8 with nothing else held.
  2. That pattern takes over at once, at the same step position (modulo its length).
NotePatterns whose lengths are multiples of each other stay in step.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide10
17

Play Direction

Sequencer playPlaying

PLAY stays lit: the arpeggio LEDs do not show the direction.

  1. While playing, press UP (forward), DOWN (backward), U&D (ping-pong), or DOWN and U&D together (random).
  2. The direction is kept across power-off.
NoteConfig menu keys 15-18 set it too.
18

Shift

Sequencer playPlaying

PLAY lit; the pattern moved one step ahead of the clock.

  1. While playing, hold KEY TRANSPOSE.
  2. UP plays the next step at once, DOWN the previous one: the pattern moves against the clock.

19

Loop Length, Live

Sequencer playPlaying

Key 2 with HOLD held: only the first 2 steps loop.

  1. While playing, hold the HOLD button and press key n: only the first n steps loop (key 1 = 1 step).
  2. The same key again: the full pattern.
NoteNothing is erased. The HOLD pedal does not do this; it keeps its hold (or transpose) job.

Transpose and Tune
20

Key Transpose

Any mode but LOADAny time

KEY TRANSPOSE lit: transposed up 4 semitones (key 12 = E).

  1. Hold KEY TRANSPOSE and press a key: everything that plays moves by its distance from the lowest C (key 8). The LED stays lit.
  2. It stays after you let go: keyboard, arpeggio and sequencer all follow.
  3. KEY TRANSPOSE + key 8 returns to normal and the LED goes out.
NoteUnlike the stock SH-101 the transpose also reaches CV OUT and MIDI OUT.
21

Transpose Mode

Sequencer play or held arpeggioAny time

KEY TRANSPOSE blinking: transpose mode. Key 10 moved the pattern up 2.

  1. Tap KEY TRANSPOSE twice quickly: its LED blinks.
  2. While the sequencer plays, or an arpeggio runs latched by HOLD, plain keys transpose: no button to hold.
  3. Double-tap again to leave; the last transpose stays.
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide13
22

Pedal as Key Transpose

Config menu, then any modeSet once

Pedal down + key 12 after key 19: KEY TRANSPOSE lit, transposed.

  1. In the config menu press key 19: the HOLD pedal jack now acts as the KEY TRANSPOSE button.
  2. Hold the pedal and press a key to transpose; double-tap it for transpose mode; in LOAD it enters a rest.
  3. Key 19 again in the menu: back to HOLD.
NoteKept across power-off. The HOLD button keeps working as HOLD.
23

Tune

KeyboardKeyboard mode

LOAD blinking: the tune note is sounding.

  1. From keyboard mode, hold KEY TRANSPOSE and press LOAD: the LOAD LED blinks and an A sounds with the gate open.
  2. At 8' with TRANSPOSE on M it is 440 Hz; MIDI OUT sends note 69 for a tuner.
  3. Set TUNE, then press any function button to leave.

Config Menu
24

Enter and Leave

Config menuAny time

Menu: HOLD lit; PLAY lit = SuperOS is running.

  1. Hold HOLD and KEY TRANSPOSE together for 2 seconds (or while switching on).
  2. The HOLD LED lights, the gate closes, and LOAD (80C49 emulator) or PLAY (SuperOS) shows the firmware that is running. KEY TRANSPOSE lit = MIDI clock sync on.
  3. Press KEY TRANSPOSE to leave. Every setting is kept across power-off.
25

Firmware: SuperOS or Original

Config menuIn the menu

PLAY lit: SuperOS is running.

  1. In the menu, LOAD restarts into the 80C49 emulator: the original Roland program.
  2. PLAY restarts into SuperOS.
  3. The choice is kept; the menu works the same way in both.
NoteIn emulator mode the stock SH-101 behaves as from the factory (100 steps, stock arpeggio), with MIDI and clock sync added. Its power-on test mode (LOAD + KEY TRANSPOSE) works there.
26

MIDI Clock Sync

Config menuIn the menu

KEY TRANSPOSE lit: MIDI clock sync is on.

  1. In the menu, press U&D to switch MIDI clock sync on or off.
  2. KEY TRANSPOSE lit = on. The arpeggio and sequencer then follow MIDI clock (MIDI Sync).
SuperOS-101 · Shortcut Guide15
27

Clock Division

Config menuIn the menu

Key 7: one step per 1/16 (the default).

  1. In the menu, press one of keys 1-10 for the step length: 1/4, 1/4 dotted, 1/4 triplet, 1/8, 1/8 dotted, 1/8 triplet, 1/16, 1/16 dotted, 1/16 triplet, 1/32.
  2. Or step through them with UP and DOWN.
NoteNo LED shows the division. It applies with MIDI sync on.
28

Accent, Cutoff, LFO Sync

Config menuIn the menu

Key 12: TB-303 accent model.

  1. Key 11 / 12: accent model, MC-202 or TB-303 style, for notes with a high MIDI velocity (on the filter and the VCA).
  2. Key 13: MIDI filter cutoff (CC 74) on / off.
  3. Key 14: LFO hard-sync on / off. On: with MIDI sync the LFO is reset to the clock. Off: the LFO runs free for modulation while the steps still follow the clock.
NoteAccent and cutoff need the CV DAC (board v1.1 and later). No LED shows these settings.

29

Direction and Pedal

Config menuIn the menu

Key 17: ping-pong.

  1. Keys 15-18 set the sequencer direction: forward, backward, ping-pong, random (the same setting as the buttons while playing).
  2. Key 19 switches the pedal jack between HOLD and KEY TRANSPOSE.
30

Sync Delay

Config menuIn the menu

Key 24: 8 ms later than the clock.

  1. Keys 20-31 set how much later than the incoming MIDI clock the SH-101 plays: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18, 21, 25 or 30 ms.
  2. Use it to line the SH-101 up with slower gear or a delayed listening path.
NoteOnly with MIDI sync on. The editor can set 0 to 60 ms.

MIDI Sync
31

Host Transport

Sequencer, sync onMIDI clock sync on

PLAY lit: the host's Start started the sequencer.

  1. With sync on, MIDI Start starts the sequencer from the first clock, MIDI Stop stops it.
  2. Pressing PLAY yourself only arms it: the first step waits for the host's clock.
  3. A synced SH-101 never runs on its own: no clock, no steps. The arpeggio follows the clock too.
NoteStart does not interrupt a running arpeggio or LOAD. With sync off, Start only rewinds a pattern that is already playing.

MIDI

Notes 53 to 84 on the receive channel act as keys 1 to 32 (they arpeggiate and record); other notes 36 to 99 play the voice. Program change 0 to 7 selects pattern 1 to 8. Everything played goes out on MIDI OUT. The web editor (tools/web-editor) edits patterns and settings over USB-C or DIN.

About these pages

The LEDs in every drawing are what the SuperOS-101 firmware (rev 9bc8c44*) shows in the host simulator (sim/superos101-sim, the real firmware source) after the keys of that function, on a fresh machine, drawn on a photo of a production SH-101 (lit LEDs glow red; a blinking LED is half lit inside a dashed ring). Most drawings show the button strip, controllers and keyboard. Details: README.md.
